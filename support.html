Common questions

  • My career didn't finish — did I lose it? No. The game saves your run automatically; reopen the app and it resumes where you left off.
  • What's the difference between Scout and Lucky Draw? Scout lets you choose the attribute first and pick from eight legends; Lucky Draw spins a random legend and you choose what to steal from them.
  • What does Expert difficulty do? You draft without seeing ratings — and earn a +3 bonus to your final GOAT score for the risk.
  • Does the app collect my data? No — nothing, ever. See the privacy policy.
