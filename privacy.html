Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 July 2026

Tennis - GOAT Roulette ("the app") is a single-player game published by ZJ Games.

Data collection

The app collects no data whatsoever.

  • No personal information, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
  • Your player name and game progress are stored only on your device and never leave it.
  • The app has no accounts and communicates with no servers.
  • The app contains no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future update ever changes what the app collects (for example, if advertising is added), this policy will be updated first and the change will be reflected in the App Store listing.
